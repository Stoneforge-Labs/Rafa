# Responsible Use & Compliance

Rafa is intended to support **lawful planning, engineering, production, operations, governance, and investment diligence**. It is not intended to facilitate fraud, deception, unauthorized access, evasion of legal obligations, infringement, or other unlawful conduct.

## Intended use

Use Rafa to help people and organizations:

- plan and document legitimate cinematic, virtual-production, software, and operational work;
- define responsibilities, approvals, evidence, testing, recovery, and governance;
- evaluate technical and business decisions using explicit assumptions and stage gates;
- improve security, privacy, rights management, reliability, and accountability;
- prepare lawful internal, commercial, investor, and implementation materials.

## Prohibited or unsupported use

Do not use this repository to:

- commit or facilitate fraud, false statements, deceptive fundraising, or misrepresentation;
- obtain unauthorized access to accounts, systems, networks, data, devices, or facilities;
- bypass authentication, licensing, access controls, sanctions, export controls, payment obligations, or other lawful restrictions;
- conceal material facts from investors, customers, employees, regulators, auditors, insurers, or contractual counterparties;
- infringe copyright, trademark, publicity, performer, voice, privacy, biometric, data-protection, or other third-party rights;
- process, disclose, sell, or train on data when the organization lacks the required rights, consent, authority, or lawful basis;
- impersonate another person or organization or falsify approvals, evidence, logs, costs, results, credentials, signatures, or records;
- use security, infrastructure, or automation examples against systems without the owner's explicit authorization;
- use the documentation as a substitute for required professional advice, permits, licenses, certifications, contractual approvals, or regulatory review.

## Compliance responsibilities

Anyone implementing or relying on Rafa is responsible for determining and following the rules that apply to their actual activity, including where relevant:

- corporate, securities, fundraising, accounting, tax, and financial-reporting requirements;
- employment, contractor-classification, accessibility, workplace, and health-and-safety requirements;
- privacy, biometric, identity, data-protection, retention, deletion, and breach-notification requirements;
- copyright, trademark, publicity, performer, music, voice, model/provider, software, and content-license terms;
- cybersecurity, computer-access, communications, surveillance, and incident-response laws;
- export controls, sanctions, anti-bribery, anti-corruption, and trade restrictions;
- advertising, consumer-protection, competition, and public-claims rules;
- customer, partner, vendor, insurance, financing, and other contractual obligations.

Laws and contractual obligations vary by jurisdiction and can change. When a decision has legal, tax, accounting, securities, employment, privacy, safety, or regulatory consequences, obtain advice from a qualified professional in the applicable jurisdiction.

## Truthful claims and investor materials

The business and investment documents in this repository contain **planning assumptions and illustrative scenarios**. They must not be presented as audited results, guaranteed outcomes, verified market facts, committed customer demand, or actual financing terms unless independently supported by current evidence.

Before external use:

1. distinguish historical facts from forecasts, targets, assumptions, and examples;
2. verify financial figures, customer claims, technical performance, rights, and milestones;
3. identify material risks, dependencies, and limitations;
4. obtain required corporate, board, legal, accounting, securities, and contractual approvals;
5. retain evidence supporting material public or investor-facing claims.

## Rights, consent, and data

Rafa's intended model is that rights and restrictions travel with governed assets. Do not assume that possession of a file, prompt, recording, scan, likeness, voice, model output, dataset, or credential creates permission to use it.

Use only assets and data for which the organization has a documented lawful basis and the required rights. Respect consent, license scope, territory, purpose, retention, revocation, deletion, attribution, confidentiality, and training restrictions.

## Security authorization

Examples involving networking, identity, automation, incident response, access control, or testing are for authorized defensive and implementation work. Nothing in this repository grants permission to test, scan, access, disrupt, modify, or collect data from systems you do not own or have explicit authorization to assess.

See [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) for the repository's security policy.

## No professional-advice relationship

This repository provides planning and technical documentation. It does not create an attorney-client, accountant-client, investment-adviser, fiduciary, employment-adviser, or other regulated professional relationship.
